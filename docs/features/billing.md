# Billing

Manage your Lattice subscription and billing details.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Community | Free | WireGuard mesh, iptables ACLs, basic dashboard |
| Pro | See [pricing](https://lattice.io/pricing) | eBPF enforcement, time-travel debugging, compliance reports, advanced audit |

## Managing Your Subscription

1. Go to **User → Billing** from the sidebar
2. View current plan and usage
3. Upgrade to Pro for advanced features
4. Download invoices and payment history
